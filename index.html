Victor Ainza
★★★★★
Backyard went from blah to WOW!!!

My wife and I wanted to make better use of a portion of our backyard and transform it into something both practical and enjoyable. To create more usable space facing our swimming pool, we decided to remove a built-in grill island that I had constructed in the early 2000s. This opened up the area and gave us the opportunity to create a true outdoor living and entertainment space rather than simply maintaining a patch of grass. After many discussions about our vision for the space, we decided on a wood deck topped with a pergola. Our goal was to create a dedicated entertainment and relaxation area overlooking the shallow end of the pool, where family and friends could gather and enjoy the outdoors. As with any project I take on, I devoted a significant amount of time to research. I reviewed hundreds of photos, studied landscaping concepts, compared deck designs, and explored numerous options before making a decision. After extensive evaluation, I chose A-DIY as the deck provider for several key reasons: competitive pricing, the kit-based assembly system, the warranty, and the fact that the product was engineered by professionals. I was particularly impressed by the thoughtful design. Everything arrived pre-measured, pre-cut, pre-drilled, and complete with all the necessary hardware, making the process incredibly efficient. The ordering process was straightforward and user-friendly. I reviewed the available layouts and dimensions, selected the design that best suited our space, and was pleasantly surprised to receive a discount as well. A few weeks after placing the order, three pallets of materials arrived. I reviewed the plans and overall design concept with my handyman so he could fully understand the intended look and layout. He was immediately impressed by the quality of the materials and confidently stated that the deck could be assembled in days rather than weeks. He was absolutely right. The entire project took approximately eight days to complete. Along the way, we incorporated a few custom touches, including a built-in step and wrap-around skirting to prevent animals from nesting beneath the deck. Since completing the deck and pergola, we've hosted several gatherings with family and friends. The feedback has been overwhelmingly positive: “Oh my God, that's beautiful!” “That came from a kit?” “It took less than 10 days to build?” “This looks like something you'd see at a resort!” My wife and I couldn't agree more. We are absolutely thrilled with our decision to choose an A-DIY deck. From the initial consultation and material selection process to delivery and assembly, every aspect of the experience exceeded our expectations. As an engineer myself, I have a deep appreciation for good design and thoughtful execution. The engineering, material quality, packaging, instructions, logistics, and overall assembly process reflect a tremendous amount of planning and attention to detail. Kudos to the entire A-DIY team, from the designers and manufacturing staff to the shipping and customer service teams, for creating such an exceptional product. The finished deck has completely transformed our backyard. Not only does it look incredible, but with the durability of the Trex materials, we're confident it will provide years of enjoyment and stand the test of time. Thank you, A-DIY, for delivering an outstanding product, a seamless ordering experience, and excellent customer service. We couldn't be happier with the result.
